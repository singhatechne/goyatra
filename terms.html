Terms, Privacy & Vehicle Booking

Review our policies and book a vehicle for your special occasion

Terms & Conditions for Vehicle Booking

Welcome to GoYatra. These terms govern your use of our vehicle booking services for buses, jeeps, and hiace vans. By making a booking, you agree to these terms.

Last Updated: January 1, 2025

1. Booking Confirmation

All bookings are confirmed upon receipt of full payment or advance deposit as specified. A confirmation email with booking details will be sent to your registered email.

2. Vehicle Types and Capacity

We offer three primary vehicle types:

  • Bus: Suitable for large groups, tours, and events (up to 40 passengers).
  • Jeep: Ideal for off-road, mountain trips, or smaller groups (up to 7 passengers).
  • Hiace: Comfortable for medium groups, airport transfers, and family trips (up to 14 passengers).

3. Occasions and Usage

Our vehicles can be booked for various purposes including tours, marriages, corporate events, religious pilgrimages, and personal travel. Please specify the occasion during booking to ensure proper vehicle arrangement.

4. Rental Period and Dates

Vehicles can be rented for single or multiple days. The rental period begins at the agreed pickup time and ends upon return. Additional hours may incur extra charges.

5. Payment Terms

A 50% advance deposit is required to secure the booking. The remaining balance must be paid before the start of the journey. We accept eSewa, Khalti, and major credit/debit cards.

6. Cancellation and Refund Policy

  • Cancellations made 7 days or more before departure: Full refund of advance.
  • Cancellations between 3-7 days: 50% refund of advance.
  • Less than 3 days: No refund.

7. Customer Responsibilities

Customers are responsible for providing accurate information, adhering to vehicle capacity, and ensuring that the vehicle is used for lawful purposes. Any damage caused by passengers will be charged accordingly.

8. Insurance and Liability

All our vehicles are insured. However, GoYatra is not liable for personal belongings left in vehicles or for delays caused by unforeseen circumstances.

9. Changes to Booking

Any changes to booking dates or vehicle type must be communicated at least 48 hours in advance. Changes are subject to availability.

10. Governing Law

These terms are governed by the laws of Nepal.

Privacy Policy

GoYatra values your privacy. This policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your personal information when you book a vehicle.

Last Updated: January 1, 2025

1. Information We Collect

  • Personal Details: Name, contact number, email address.
  • Booking Details: Vehicle type, date, duration, pickup/drop locations, occasion.
  • Payment Information: Transaction details for processing.
  • Technical Data: IP address, browser type, device information.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To process and confirm your vehicle booking.
  • To communicate about your booking status.
  • To provide customer support.
  • To improve our services and website.

3. Information Sharing

We may share your information with:

  • Drivers or vehicle operators assigned to your trip.
  • Payment processors for transaction completion.
  • Legal authorities if required by law.

We do not sell your personal data.

4. Data Security

We implement appropriate security measures to protect your personal information.

5. Data Retention

We retain your data only as long as necessary for the purposes stated.

6. Your Rights

You may request access, correction, or deletion of your personal data at any time.

7. Cookies

We use cookies to enhance user experience. You can manage cookie preferences via the settings.

8. Contact

For privacy concerns, contact us at privacy@goyatra.com.
